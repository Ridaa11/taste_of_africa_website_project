Injera

A spongy, sour flatbread made from teff and used as plate and spoon for stews like doro wat.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups teff flour
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

Step 1

Whisk teff flour and water into a smooth batter.

Step 2

Cover and ferment at room temperature for 2 to 3 days until bubbly and sour.

Step 3

Stir in salt and thin the batter to a pourable consistency.

Step 4

Pour in a spiral onto a hot non-stick pan. Cover and cook 2 minutes, without flipping.

Step 5

Cool on a clean towel before stacking.
