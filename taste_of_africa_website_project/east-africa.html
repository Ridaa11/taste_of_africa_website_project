East Africa

Fermented grains, lentil stews and coastal coconut curries define the region. Berbere spice blends and cardamom reflect centuries of Indian Ocean trade.

Staples

Teff, maize (ugali), lentils, plantain, coconut

Cooking techniques

Fermenting, simmering, pan-baking flatbreads, charcoal grilling (nyama choma)
