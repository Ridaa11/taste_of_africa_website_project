North Africa

Mediterranean, Arab and Amazigh traditions meet in sweet-savoury cooking: cumin, saffron and harissa flavour grains, lamb, chickpeas and preserved lemons.

Staples

Couscous, wheat, chickpeas, dates, olives

Cooking techniques

Slow-cooking in clay pots, steaming, spice-rubbing, baking in communal ovens
