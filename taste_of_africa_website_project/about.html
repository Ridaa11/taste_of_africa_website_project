About us

Our mission is to keep African food heritage alive: documenting recipes, honouring the people who cook them, and sharing them with the world.

A short history

African cuisine grew from indigenous grains like sorghum, millet and teff, then absorbed spices and techniques from Arab, Indian Ocean, European and Atlantic trade. The movement of people carried jollof, okra stews and groundnut sauces to the Americas and the Caribbean, shaping Southern US, Brazilian and Caribbean cooking.
