West Africa

Cooking here is built on palm oil, scotch bonnet peppers, groundnuts and fermented seasonings. Starchy staples like yam, cassava and rice meet bold, layered stews.

Staples

Rice, yam, cassava, plantain, millet

Cooking techniques

Frying, slow stewing, pounding (fufu), open-fire grilling (suya)
